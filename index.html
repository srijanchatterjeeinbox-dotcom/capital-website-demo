FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Capital Excellence Institute and which exams does it prepare for?
A concept-first coaching institute in Greater Noida, established in 2025, offering offline programs for IIT-JEE (Main & Advanced), NEET (UG), Pre-Foundation (Classes 8, 9 and 10) and Olympiads.
Where is the institute located?
1st Floor, Above ICICI Bank, Kaveri City Centre, Delta 1, Near Delta 1 Metro Station, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh – 201310. It has excellent metro connectivity from Noida, Greater Noida, Knowledge Park and Pari Chowk.
What makes Capital Excellence Institute different?
Mentorship from IITians, doctors and NIT gold medalists; a concept-first approach over rote learning; limited batch sizes; regular tests with performance analysis; and a disciplined, distraction-free campus.
What facilities are available for students?
Air-conditioned digital smart classrooms, regular tests with performance tracking, doubt-clearing sessions, a 24-hour library, transport facility, a safe and hygienic campus and metro connectivity.
Does the institute conduct regular tests?
Yes — weekly topic tests, monthly performance tests and full-length mocks on the real exam pattern, each followed by a detailed analysis report.
How can I apply for admission?
Fill the 30-second enquiry form, get a call back from our counsellor, then visit the campus to plan your preparation. You can also call or WhatsApp us. The enquiry needs no registration and no fees.
